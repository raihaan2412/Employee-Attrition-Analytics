# Employee Attrition Analytics — Key Insights

## Overall Situation

- Total Employees: 1,470
- Employees Left: 237
- Overall Attrition Rate: 16.12%
- Average Years at Company: 7.01
- Average Monthly Income: $6,502.93

## Major Attrition Patterns

### 1. Overtime
- Employees working overtime have 30.53% attrition.
- Employees not working overtime have 10.44% attrition.
- Chi-square test shows a statistically significant association with attrition.

### 2. Early Tenure
- Employees with 0–2 years at the company have 29.82% attrition.
- This is considerably higher than employees with longer tenure.

### 3. Job Role
- Sales Representatives have the highest attrition at 39.76%.
- Laboratory Technicians have 23.94% attrition.
- Research Directors have the lowest attrition at 2.50%.

### 4. Department
- Sales has the highest department-level attrition at 20.63%.
- Human Resources has 19.05%.
- Research & Development has 13.84%.

### 5. Income
- Low-income employees have 29.27% attrition.
- Very-high-income employees have 10.33% attrition.

### 6. Work Conditions
- Low job involvement has 33.73% attrition.
- Low work-life balance has 31.25% attrition.
- Low job satisfaction has 22.84% attrition.
- Employees who travel frequently have 24.91% attrition.

## Statistical Findings

Chi-square tests showed statistically significant associations between
attrition and:

- Overtime
- Job Satisfaction
- Work-Life Balance
- Job Involvement
- Business Travel

All tested factors had p-values below 0.05.

## Recommended Actions

### Reduce Overtime Pressure
- Monitor employees working frequent overtime.
- Review workload distribution.
- Encourage healthier working schedules.

### Focus on Early-Tenure Employees
- Strengthen onboarding and mentoring.
- Regularly check new employees' experience.
- Identify early signs of disengagement.

### Focus on High-Risk Roles
- Investigate retention issues among Sales Representatives.
- Review workload, incentives and career growth opportunities.

### Improve Employee Experience
- Monitor job satisfaction and work-life balance.
- Improve employee involvement in day-to-day work.

### Review Compensation
- Examine retention challenges among lower-income employees.
- Evaluate compensation and career progression opportunities.

## Important Limitation

This dataset does not contain employee hire dates or exit dates.
Therefore, monthly or annual attrition rates cannot be calculated reliably.

The analysis identifies associations and patterns in the dataset.
It does not prove that a single factor directly causes employee attrition.
